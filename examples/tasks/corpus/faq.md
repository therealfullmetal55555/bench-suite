# Support FAQ

The answers that support gives most often, in the words they use.

## Refund window

Refunds are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Outside that window the order is not
eligible, and support cannot make an exception.

## Shipping estimates

Standard shipping is 3–5 working days; express is next working day if the order was
placed before 14:00.

## Order changes

An order can be changed until it is picked. After that it can only be returned.
