# Support FAQ

Everything a support agent is allowed to tell a customer, in one file. The corpus is
public with the tasks, on purpose: the reference answers are in the documents, and the
question is whether the agent can find them and quote them — not whether it has read the
same Slack thread the task author did.

## Refund window

A customer can ask for a refund within 30 days of delivery. The clock starts when the
carrier marks the parcel delivered, not when the order was placed: an order that sat in a
depot for a week still gets its full 30 days. Past that window a refund is not normally
available, and an agent that grants one anyway has spent the company's money on a promise
it cannot keep.

## Damaged orders

A damaged order is not bound by the refund window. The customer may choose a replacement or
a refund, and the choice is theirs, not the agent's. Damage claims have no time limit
because the alternative — telling somebody their broken parcel is 34 days old and therefore
their problem — costs more in support time than the refund does.

## Shipping estimates

Estimates are quoted in business days and exclude the day the order is placed. An order
placed on a Friday with a 3-day estimate arrives on Wednesday. Agents do not quote a date;
they quote the range the carrier gave, because a promise with a date on it is a promise that
gets escalated when a van breaks down.

## Order changes

An order can be changed until it is picked: the address, the shipping speed, or the items.
After picking, the only options are to cancel and re-order, which the customer has to decide
because it changes the delivery date. An agent that edits a picked order produces a parcel
that goes to the old address and a support ticket that nobody can close.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human when the customer has asked twice for the same thing and the answer has
not changed, when the request involves an account the agent cannot read, or when the
customer is a business account. Escalating earlier is not caution; it is queue time for
somebody else, and two escalations on the same order is the sign the agent should have
finished the job itself.
